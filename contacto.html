ABOUT US & THE FOUNDER

Hi there,

My name is Kisrak. I’m the founder of BOHEMIAN 20s, and I started this community because I’m in my twenties, and I refuse to live a life scripted by someone else. I don't want to be trapped. I want to be free, and I want to genuinely live.

For the longest time, I found myself caught in the endless loop of working hard just to survive—only to realize that the harder I tried to get ahead in this world, the less alive I actually felt. My biggest dream is to be able to see the world before it gets destroyed, to experience new cultures, meet new people, and fill my days with art, curiosity, and real human connection instead of staring at screens.

Let's be honest: some days are really difficult. We watch our generation influenced by beautiful video edits of trips with friends, wondering how we can ever live that out when we're buried in responsibilities. But I've learned that the moments I feel most alive and breathing are when I'm out on a trip, meeting people who are just like me—or completely different—and feeling that spark of shared adventure.

I want to have more positive days than negative days. If you're a dreamer who feels a little stuck and wants a crew to explore the wild with, you are in the right place. We just got to do it.

— Kisrak, Founder of Bohemian 20s 🌲
